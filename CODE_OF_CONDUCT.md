# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Discuss code, not the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about trade-offs on real networks, not about anyone's competence.
- Be specific. "This adds a query per row on large networks" helps; "this is bad code" does not.
- Welcome questions, including basic ones. Multisite is genuinely confusing, and nobody is born knowing that cron is per site.
- Accept that the answer to a feature request may be no, and that a maintainer saying "this is out of scope" is not a personal judgement.
- Report security issues privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) rather than publishing them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal, political or identity-based attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information — including data pulled from a network you have access to.
- Deliberately derailing issues or pull requests, or repeatedly reopening a settled decision.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any other project space, in public and in private.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts that do not follow this document. Serious or repeated behaviour leads to a permanent ban.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately rather than posting about it publicly. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared with the person being reported unless they agree to it.

Maintainers are held to this document too — more strictly, not less.
